# Creative Thinking (Syniq compulsory)

## Use

- Stuck on conventional solution
- UX, naming, positioning, or narrative tasks
- Need 2–3 distinct directions before converging

## Method

1. Diverge — 3 ideas, no self-censor (short bullets)
2. Converge — pick best against constraints
3. Stress-test — failure modes of chosen idea

## Balance

Creativity serves the user goal — not novelty for its own sake. Pair with analytical-thinking to filter ideas.

Skip heavy ideation on straightforward implementation tasks.
