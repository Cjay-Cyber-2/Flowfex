# Self-Improving Compound

## Model

Each turn adds a small improvement that **compounds**:

- Turn 1: fix clarity
- Turn 2: fix Syniq skill selection
- Turn 3: fix verification habit
- Net: faster, safer, clearer assistance

## Practices

- Log one compound habit per turn (internal)
- Reuse successful patterns; retire failed ones
- Do not repeat full re-explanation of stable context

## Syniq

Complements compulsory better-every-run and self-iteration-engine; optional depth for long sessions.
