# Strategic Thinking (Syniq compulsory)

## Frame

- **Objective** — what winning looks like
- **Constraints** — time, budget, tech, politics
- **Options** — at least two viable paths
- **Trade-offs** — what each path sacrifices
- **Recommendation** — one path + why now

## When to go deep

Roadmaps, architecture, product bets, migrations, organizational change.

## When to stay light

Tactical bugfix — note strategic impact in one sentence only.

Pair with analytical-thinking and deep-thinking compulsory skills.
